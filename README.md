# Custom AI Characters

**Create your characters. Shape their personalities. Bring their stories to life.**

Custom AI Characters is a Windows desktop application for creating, managing, and chatting with your own AI characters.

Built with local AI through Ollama, the application gives you control over your characters and conversations while also offering a community Discover experience.

## Features

- **Character Creation:** Build characters with customizable appearances, personalities, backgrounds, speaking styles, scenarios, and more.
- **AI-Assisted Creation:** Use AI to help generate and refine individual character profile sections.
- **Character Library:** Organize, favorite, archive, duplicate, and manage your characters.
- **Conversations:** Have conversations with your characters, manage chat history, and create alternate response options.
- **Local AI:** Connect to Ollama and run supported AI models locally.
- **Discover:** Explore and publish shareable characters through the community.
- **Privacy:** Your personal characters and conversations are stored locally on your computer.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- [Ollama](https://ollama.com/) installed
- A supported Ollama model downloaded
- An internet connection for account and community features

## Installation

1. Download the latest Windows installer from the [Releases](../../releases) page.
2. Run the installer and follow the instructions.
3. Install Ollama if you haven't already.
4. Download one of the supported AI models.
5. Launch Custom AI Characters and get started.

## Supported AI Models

| Model | Description |
|---|---|
| Llama 3.2 | Lightweight |
| Gemma 3 | Balanced |
| Qwen 3 | Higher Quality |

Model availability and performance depend on your computer's hardware.
